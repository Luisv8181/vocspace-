# Ethics, Safety, and Guardrails

VocSpace works near disability, employment, health information, legal rights, and potentially sensitive personal circumstances.

The platform therefore needs unusually strong boundaries.

## Never present AI output as a determination

VocSpace should not independently determine:

- disability status
- legal entitlement
- medical diagnosis
- fitness for duty
- employability
- causation
- legal compliance

AI can support reasoning and preparation while appropriately deferring final determinations to qualified humans and authoritative sources.

## Provenance

Every substantive recommendation should be traceable to:

- a source
- a design rationale
- an implementation assumption
- a validation method

## Human agency

The person experiencing the barrier should remain central.

The system should not ask only:

> What is wrong with you?

It should ask:

> What are you trying to do, what is getting in the way, and what would make participation easier?

## Privacy

Prototype with synthetic cases whenever possible.

Avoid collecting health/disability details unless necessary for a user-selected workflow.

Separate identity from case information where practical.

## Outcomes

Recommendations should not become “truth” merely because an AI generated them.

A solution becomes stronger when there is evidence that it worked for the person in context.

## Accessibility

VocSpace must meet a high accessibility bar itself. Every feature should be evaluated as a possible accommodation or barrier generator.
