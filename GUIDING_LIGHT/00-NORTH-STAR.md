# VocSpace Guiding Light

## North Star

VocSpace is a living laboratory for Clinical Rehabilitation Counseling innovation.

It explores how rehabilitation counseling principles can become software, open-source tools, educational resources, accessibility infrastructure, and human-centered AI.

The central belief:

> Accessibility is not simply a compliance problem. Accommodation is not simply a form. The goal is meaningful participation.

VocSpace should help people move from:

**“I have a barrier.”**

to:

**“I understand the barrier, I can describe the functional need, I can see possible solutions, I can connect with the right resource or person, I can build or request the solution, and I can determine whether it actually worked.”**

This is the guiding light for every future feature.
