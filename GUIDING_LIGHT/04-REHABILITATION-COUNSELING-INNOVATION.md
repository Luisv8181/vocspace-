# VocSpace as a Clinical Rehabilitation Counseling Innovation Lab

VocSpace should preserve the professional identity of its creator: a Clinical Rehabilitation Counselor interested in translating counseling theory and rehabilitation practice into useful technology.

## Rehabilitation counseling lens

Core concepts should remain visible throughout the platform:

- person-centered practice
- participation
- function
- environment
- work demands
- strengths
- barriers
- supports
- self-determination
- informed choice
- disability identity
- vocational development
- accommodations
- assistive technology
- rehabilitation outcomes

A feature belongs in VocSpace when it helps translate one or more of these concepts into action.

### Research

VocSpace can also become a research testbed.

Potential research questions:

- Which accommodation patterns work for which functional barriers?
- How accurately can AI translate narratives into functional needs?
- Can users design their own accommodations more effectively with visual primitives?
- Which AI-generated accessibility interventions are usable in practice?
- How should outcomes be represented without reducing people to diagnoses?
- How can lived experience improve accommodation design datasets?
- How can AI tools support, rather than replace, counselor judgment?

## Guiding principle

The platform should never turn rehabilitation counseling into a checkbox exercise.

The software should support professional reasoning, client agency, experimentation, and participation.
