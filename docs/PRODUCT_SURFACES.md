# VocSpace Product Surfaces

The platform should eventually be thought of as a family of connected surfaces rather than one giant dashboard.

## 1. Barrier Explorer

Describe or visualize a barrier.

## 2. Accommodation Studio

Explore and design potential accommodations.

## 3. Accessibility Lab

Interact with accessible interface patterns and compare implementations.

## 4. AI Accommodation Builder

Turn natural language or symbolic inputs into a proposed digital accommodation or implementation plan.

## 5. Resource Navigator

Find people, tools, organizations, guidance, and services based on capabilities.

## 6. Request Studio

Prepare an accommodation request, implementation request, or stakeholder communication.

## 7. Solution Compiler

Turn the selected intervention into implementation artifacts for humans and coding agents.

## 8. Outcome Tracker

Evaluate whether the solution worked.

## 9. Vocational Workspace

Original VocSpace career, vocational rehabilitation, assessment, work, and self-advocacy concepts.

## 10. Professional Workspace

Tools for counselors, vocational experts, educators, accessibility professionals, and researchers.

## 11. Research Lab

Synthetic cases, experiments, benchmarks, prototypes, and findings.

These surfaces should share a common underlying model rather than becoming disconnected mini-applications.
